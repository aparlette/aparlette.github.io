**Last updated: 6 August 2026**

Interstate Tracker records which parts of the U.S. Interstate Highway System you
have driven. Doing that requires your location. This policy describes what the
app does with it.

The short version: everything stays on your phone. The app has no permission to
access the internet, so it cannot send your data anywhere, and it does not.

## What the app accesses

**Location.** With your permission, the app reads your device's GPS location,
including while it is in the background, so it can record a drive without you
having to keep the app open. Location is used to match your route against
interstate geometry that ships inside the app.

**Physical activity.** With your permission, the app reads Android's activity
recognition signal to detect automatically when you have started or stopped
driving. You can decline this and start and stop trips manually instead.

**Notifications.** While a trip is recording, the app shows an ongoing
notification, as Android requires for an app using location in the background.

## Where that data goes

Nowhere. It is stored in the app's private storage on your device and is never
transmitted.

This is not only a policy commitment. The app does not request the Android
`INTERNET` permission, so the operating system prevents it from making any
network connection at all. There is no account to create, no server to sync
with, and no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service in the app.

Because your data never leaves your device, there is nobody to sell it to, and
no third party receives it.

## Google Play services

The app uses Google Play services on your device for location and activity
recognition. These are Android system components, not part of Interstate
Tracker. Any processing Google performs through them is governed by Google's own
privacy policy, at <https://policies.google.com/privacy>. Interstate Tracker
does not send Google, or anyone else, the data it stores.

## Data you choose to export

The app can export your trips and coverage as a file, and can import GPX files
you already have. Exporting is always something you initiate. Once you have
saved or shared an exported file, it is an ordinary file on your device and
outside the app's control — where it goes next is up to you.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete individual
trips from within the app. Uninstalling Interstate Tracker deletes everything it
stored, permanently; because there is no server copy, this cannot be undone and
there is nothing left behind to request the deletion of.

## Children

Interstate Tracker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly
collect information from them.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it does with your data — for
example by adding optional accounts or cloud sync — this policy will be updated
before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
