NogLog is a logbook for eggnog: browse the brands, record a tasting, write the
review, keep your favorites, and share a homemade recipe. There is a web app
and an Android build.

**It has not launched yet**, so there is nothing to sign up for at the moment.

More detail is on the studio page:
[studio.parlette.net/work/noglog](https://studio.parlette.net/work/noglog/).
