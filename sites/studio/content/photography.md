## Where the pictures are

The photographs live on Flickr, which is where they have been going for years —
including a run at the 365 discipline of making a frame every day.

- [The photostream](https://www.flickr.com/photos/9297241@N02/) — everything,
  newest first
- [New River Gorge](https://www.flickr.com/photos/9297241@N02/sets/72177720312641660)
  — a West Virginia set

A small selection also sits on the personal site, at
[andrew.parlette.net/photo-gallery](https://andrew.parlette.net/photo-gallery/).

## What is coming here

A real portfolio — a chosen, sequenced, printed-size selection rather than a
stream — belongs on this site, and it is not built yet. Choosing which frames
are portfolio-grade is not a job to hand to anyone else, so this page points at
Flickr until that edit happens.

The nearest thing to photography software on the workbench is
[LensCalc](/work/lenscalc/), five field calculators for depth of field, field
of view, exposure, crop factor, and subject size in frame.
