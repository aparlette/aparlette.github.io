Almost everything here started the same way: something worth remembering, and
nothing writing it down.

Interstate Tracker logs the roads. Pitch Chart logs the pitches. NogLog logs
the eggnog. That is not a coincidence — it is the whole disposition. The work
tends toward the same shape: capture what actually happened, keep it somewhere
durable, and give it back later in a form that is worth looking at.

Parlette Studios is the name Andrew Parlette builds under. It is a workbench,
not an agency.
