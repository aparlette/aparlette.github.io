Parlette Studios is the name **Andrew Parlette** works under for software and
photography.

It is a workbench rather than an agency: projects start here as a page on this
site, and the ones that earn it graduate to a domain of their own — which is
what [Interstate Tracker](/work/interstate-tracker/) did. The rest stay here,
described accurately, for as long as that is what they are.

Andrew coaches youth baseball in Maryland; that side of things lives at
[andrew.parlette.net](https://andrew.parlette.net/), and it explains why a
noticeable share of the workbench points at a ball field.

Email reaches him at [andrew@parlette.net](mailto:andrew@parlette.net).
