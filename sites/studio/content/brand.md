Parlette Studios is the name Andrew Parlette's software and photography go out
under. It is part of the parlette.net family, with an identity of its own. The
brand should read as **precise, thoughtful, creative, understated, and capable**.

The full written guide, with the reasoning behind each rule, lives with the
source files in this site's repository at `sites/studio/brand/README.md`.

## Logo

The mark is a P built on a strict construction. A hidden A is cut into its
foot, and a thin aperture ring surrounds it, split into four blades by cuts
that run tangent to the bowl, the way an iris's blade edges do. The P matches
PARLETTE in Midnight Navy; the ring matches STUDIOS in Steel Blue.

{{< brand-logos >}}

**Use the files as supplied.** Keep clear space equal to the width of the
mark's stem on every side. The lockup should be at least 120&nbsp;px wide and
the mark at least 24&nbsp;px. Below that, use the one-colour mark. Don't recolour,
stretch, outline, shadow, or rebuild the logo from a font. The tagline is never
part of the logo.

The SVGs are trimmed tight to the art. Padded PNGs are alongside them in
[/brand/logo/png/](/brand/logo/png/parlette-studios-lockup.png), and print PDFs in
[/brand/logo/pdf/](/brand/logo/pdf/parlette-studios-lockup.pdf).

## Colour

Mostly navy, slate, and white. Signal Blue is the only saturated colour, and it
is reserved for links, focus, and selection.

{{< brand-colours >}}

Steel Blue is for graphics and large type. Use Deep Slate for small secondary
text on light backgrounds, and Light Steel in place of Steel on dark ones.

These are the parlette.net family's typefaces, which tie Studios to the rest of
Andrew's sites. Headings use Archivo at weight 600 with slightly tight tracking.
Labels, like the one above, are Archivo in capitals at weight 600, set a little
wider and tracked very wide. This is the STUDIOS treatment. The wordmark itself is custom lettering, not a font.

## Icons

Line icons on a 24-unit grid with a 1.5 stroke, drawn from the mark's own
geometry rather than generic camera or code symbols.

Buttons are square-shouldered, set in the label style, and never shadowed: solid
navy for the one thing to do next, and an outline for the alternative. Sections
are divided by 1&nbsp;px Cool Gray rules rather than boxes. Where separate things
need a grid, cards are a hairline border with no shadow.

## Imagery

Photography is Andrew's own, shown with plenty of negative space and the logo
kept out of the frame. Some decorative images on this site, like the misty
backdrop on the home page, are AI-generated illustrations. They are never
presented as photography.

The watermark on finished photographs is the mark alone, white or black at low
opacity, small, in a corner: a signature, not an advertisement.
